## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-06

### Added

- research-backed white paper and public claim ledger;
- dependency-free evidence-grounded routing reference implementation;
- Routing Decision Record, delegation-contract, outcome-event, integrity-envelope, and external-prediction schemas;
- explicit select, parallel, clarify, abstain, and escalate outcomes;
- versioned evidence, policy, registry, calibration, and cost metadata;
- calibration, replay, hash-chain integrity, and evidence-quality checks;
- synthetic 42-case benchmark with baselines, risk-coverage metrics, bootstrap comparisons, and ablations;
- optional LangGraph and Langfuse adapters with compatibility tests;
- RCA and evidence-quality evaluation protocols; and
- contribution, support, security, governance, CI, and release guidance.

### Research boundary

The included benchmark is a contaminated synthetic development exercise. Version `0.1.0` makes no production-accuracy, safety, compliance, or RCA-time claim.
